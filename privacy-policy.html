2. Information we collect

The information processed depends on the features you choose to use, the permissions you grant, and the technical services integrated into the app.

2.1 Account and profile information

If account registration is available and you create an account, we may process information such as your name or username, email address, account identifier, profile details you choose to provide, and authentication-related information. Authentication credentials should be handled by the configured authentication provider; Aroundly should not store or request your plain-text password.

Purpose: creating and managing your account, authenticating you, providing account-based features, protecting accounts, and responding to account requests.

2.2 Location information

If you grant location permission and use a location-based feature, the app may access your device's approximate or precise location, depending on the permission requested by the app and your device settings. Location is used to identify or display places near you and support location-based search.

Location permission is optional unless a particular feature cannot function without it. You can deny or revoke permission in your device settings. Where supported, you can continue to search by entering a city, area or place name. We do not intend to access device location in the background unless a clearly disclosed feature requires it and the relevant permission has been obtained.

2.3 Reviews, photographs and user-submitted content

When you submit content, we may process the review, rating, comment, photograph, image, caption or other information you choose to provide. Content submitted for public display may be shown to other Aroundly users together with the profile name or other attribution displayed by the app.

Purpose: displaying user contributions, maintaining community content, moderating submissions where applicable, and responding to reports or support requests.

2.4 Business listing information

If business listing submission is available, a business owner may provide business name, category, address, description, opening hours, images, contact details and other information relevant to the listing.

Purpose: creating, reviewing, approving, maintaining and displaying business listings, and communicating with the business owner about the submission.

2.5 Payment and receipt information

If paid listing or promotional services are offered, we may process transaction-related details such as the business owner's account or contact details, amount, transaction reference, payment status and a receipt or proof of payment submitted for verification.

Do not submit a full payment card number, card security code, banking password or other authentication secret in a receipt upload or support message. If an external payment processor is used, identify that provider and explain its role here before release. Do not state that a payment provider receives information unless that integration is actually in place.

2.6 Device, app and diagnostic information

The app or its configured service providers may process technical information needed to operate and protect the service, such as device model, operating system version, app version, language, basic network or connectivity information, error reports and crash diagnostics. The exact information depends on the SDKs and services included in the published app.

Purpose: maintaining compatibility, diagnosing failures, preventing abuse, securing the service and improving reliability.

2.7 Support communications

If you contact us, we may process your email address, the contents of your message, information you choose to provide, and correspondence needed to investigate and respond to your request.
